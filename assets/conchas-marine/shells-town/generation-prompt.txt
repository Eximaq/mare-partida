Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: complete finished high-definition raster background for a playable 2D RPG map in Kaizoku Idle.
Primary request: create an original game-map adaptation of Shells Town (Cidade das Conchas) from the early East Blue arc of One Piece, strongly centered on Captain Morgan's Marine base and the execution courtyard where adult Roronoa Zoro is tied to a wooden T-shaped post. Camera: fixed elevated top-down orthographic three-quarter JRPG view with parallel projection, consistent throughout. No horizon, no sky, no vanishing point, no cinematic landscape angle. A full-bleed rectangular playable map viewed from above, with readable roofs and south-facing walls.
Composition: the Marine base and enormous open ochre-sand courtyard occupy about 70 percent of the image. The headquarters stands at the north edge, its entrance facing south toward the courtyard. Pale stone perimeter walls run down both sides. At the southern center there is a very wide gate with both wooden doors fully open and pushed out of the road; no overhead arch obstructing the view of the walking path. A broad road connects this opening continuously to a modest harbor landing and central timber pier at the bottom edge. A compact strip of small coastal-town houses with red and blue-green roofs flanks the lower approach, and turquoise sea occupies only the lowest strip. The headquarters, yard, gate and pier must all be connected by broad unbroken dry routes.

No blood, wounds, nails or graphic injury. Show him from the same elevated three-quarter camera as the environment, facing generally south. Keep him at believable game-world person scale, roughly 5 percent of total image height for his body and 7-8 percent for the post, large enough to recognize but not a giant character portrait.
